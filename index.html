About

I am a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore, advised by Jonathan Scarlett.

I received my PhD from Università degli Studi di Milano, supervised by Nicolò Cesa-Bianchi and Wouter Koolen at the Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica (CWI). During my PhD, I also worked closely with Mengxiao Zhang at the University of Iowa.

I am broadly interested in the theoretical foundations of machine learning, the development of algorithms with provable guarantees, and their applications to real-world problems. Currently, I focus on adaptive and efficient sequential decision-making in multi-agent systems, where feedback arrives asynchronously or information is distributed across agents. My research topics include:

  • Centralized Learning with Asynchronous Feedback: I develop adaptive online learning methods that integrate asynchronous feedback and exploit unknown problem structure.
  • Decentralized Learning with Distributed Feedback: I develop cooperative decision-making methods using local observations and shared information, with guarantees under communication constraints.
